# SBC 304-2024 — Stage 19 validation hardening

## Scope
- Hardened section-input validation against `null`, booleans, blank/whitespace strings, non-finite values, negative factored moment, negative provided reinforcement, and invalid effective depth.
- Numeric strings remain accepted because browser form controls commonly supply strings.
- Added an explicit policy against silently reusing EC2/ACI resistance equations or importing BS 8110 coefficients into the SBC design-code route without an explicitly selected analysis method.
- SBC calculations remain disabled. This stage does not claim compliance or calculate design capacity.

## Official references
- Official SBC 304-2024 code details page: https://sbc.gov.sa/En/BC/Pages/BuildingCode/BCDetails.aspx?codeId=304&year=2024
- Official SBC 304-2024 Code & Commentaries contents: https://sbc.gov.sa/ar/BC/Documents/tableofcontent2024/SBC%20304/SBC304_CC_241224-FA.pdf
- The published contents identify Chapter 7 as one-way slabs and Chapter 8 as two-way slabs, with sectional strength, serviceability and reinforcement detailing in their respective chapters.

## Release gate
Before enabling any SBC calculation, verify the exact clause text and applicable Saudi amendments for the 2024 edition, then add reference-tagged calculations, numerical benchmarks, boundary tests, and independent review. A contents page is not adequate evidence for equations or limits.
