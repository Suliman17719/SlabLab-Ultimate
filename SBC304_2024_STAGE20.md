# SBC 304-2024 — Stage 20 provision mapping and release gates

## Purpose
This stage improves traceability of the SBC 304-2024 implementation. It does **not** implement or certify design equations. The engine remains fail-closed and SBC remains disabled in the application.

## Official references consulted
- SBC official code portal: https://sbc.gov.sa/En/BC/Pages/BuildingCode/BCDetails.aspx?codeId=304&year=2024
- SBC 304-CC-2024 official table of contents PDF: https://sbc.gov.sa/ar/BC/Documents/tableofcontent2024/SBC%20304/SBC304_CC_241224-FA.pdf

The table of contents confirms the relevant chapter structure: Chapter 5 loads; Chapter 6 analysis; Chapter 7 one-way slabs; Chapter 8 two-way slabs; Chapter 15 slab-column joints; Chapter 19 concrete properties/durability; Chapter 20 reinforcement properties; Chapter 21 strength reduction factors; Chapter 22 sectional strength including flexure and one-/two-way shear; Chapter 24 serviceability; Chapter 25 reinforcement detailing, development and splices.

**Limitation:** the cited PDF is titled *Code & Commentaries* and is used here to establish chapter mapping only. It does not independently validate equations or Saudi-specific requirements. Before implementing a calculation, the corresponding SBC 304-2024 Code Requirements clauses, referenced standards, units and exceptions must be checked. Do not infer that SBC 304-2024 is identical to ACI 318-25 or copy equations across code engines without verification.

## Stage 20 changes
- Added a source register and implementation matrix in `sbc304-2024-engine.js`.
- Expanded chapter mapping for analysis, live-load arrangement, simplified analysis, two-way methods, slab-column joints, spacing, and development/splices.
- Updated the app registry text and Service Worker cache version.
- Added automated tests for chapter mapping, source traceability, and fail-closed behavior.

## Release policy
The SBC engine must remain disabled until each implemented feature has a verified clause citation, hand-calculated benchmark, boundary/invalid-input tests, and independent review. Passing software tests alone does not establish structural-code compliance.
